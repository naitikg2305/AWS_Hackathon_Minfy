THE OPERATIONAL QUESTION

A pressure drop is a signal.
What does it mean?

A potential leak and an operating change can trigger similar alarms. The demonstration brings telemetry, operating context and procedures into one investigation.

DEMO EVENT FP-003 False positive assessment

The pressure changed.
The context explains why.

The agent attributes the alarm to falling temperature and gas contraction in the line, with no sustained mass-balance deficit.

EVIDENCE

SCADA readings, operating context and the alarm timeline.

HUMAN REVIEW

Review the evidence and the recommendation to continue monitoring.

DEMO EVENT LK-003 Likely leak assessment

A sustained deficit.
A reason to investigate.

The agent identifies a sustained mass-balance deficit without a mechanical explanation and marks an estimated location.

EVIDENCE

Flow trends, mass balance, an estimated segment and cited procedures.

HUMAN REVIEW

The operator reviews the recommended field verification and decides the response under established procedures.

◎

Decision support, with the operator in control. The demonstration recommends next steps. It does not execute field actions or isolate the pipeline.
